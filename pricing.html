How to buy or renew

  1. Install Amanat Backup on each computer and open Help → Licence. Next to This computer's code, click Copy. The code identifies the computer, not you.
  2. Send us the edition you want, the computer code(s) and the name the licence should show, by WhatsApp or e-mail. We reply with the amount and the account details for payment.
  3. Pay by JazzCash or bank transfer and send us the payment receipt.
  4. You receive your licence key. Paste it into Help → Licence (or use Load from file…) and click Activate.

Questions about licences

What happens when the trial or a licence ends?
Amanat Backup continues as the free edition: one backup job (the oldest) and up to 25 GB per backup. Nothing is deleted, and every backup can still be restored, checked and cleaned up.
How does renewal work?
A renewal is a new key that replaces the old one. The Dashboard reminds you 30 days before the end date.
I reinstalled Windows or replaced the computer.
The computer code changes. Contact us with the new code and we will re-issue your key.
Do I need internet to activate?
No. The key is checked entirely on your computer.
