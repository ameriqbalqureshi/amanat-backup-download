Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information Mikrotronics Pakistan ("we") handles in connection with Amanat Backup: in the program itself, when you buy a licence or contact us, and on this website. In short: the program sends us nothing, and we only receive what you choose to send us.

1. The Amanat Backup program

Your files never leave your control. Amanat Backup works entirely on your computer. It does not connect to the internet, has no user accounts, and sends no files, usage statistics, crash reports or other information to us or to anyone else. The only time it uses the internet is when you click a web link in its help or contact pages, which opens in your web browser.

The program stores the following, only on your own computer and drives:

  • Your backups, in the repositories you create (for example on a USB drive). Their contents and file names are encrypted with AES-256 using a key derived from your password.
  • Settings, backup jobs, history and logs, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AmanatBackup for each Windows user.
  • The unattended-backup key, only if you allow backups without the password. It is protected by Windows for your user account and is never written to the backup drive.
  • Trial and licence information, in the settings and in the Windows registry of your user account.

Your password and recovery key are never stored or sent anywhere. That is why nobody, including us, can open your backups or reset a forgotten password. We will never ask you for your password or recovery key.

The computer code shown under Help → Licence is calculated from the Windows installation identifier of your computer using a one-way function. It does not contain your name, files or any other personal information, and the program does not send it anywhere; you send it to us yourself when you order a licence.

2. When you buy a licence or contact us

When you order a licence or ask for support (by WhatsApp, phone or e-mail), we receive the information you send, which may include:

  • your name or company name, as it should appear on the licence;
  • your phone number, WhatsApp number or e-mail address;
  • the computer code(s) the licence is for;
  • the payment receipt, which may show the name and account of the payer;
  • for support, the program details you paste (version, edition, licence) and anything else you choose to include, such as screenshots.

We use this information only to issue, renew and re-issue licences, to answer your questions, to keep a record of the licences we have issued (licence number, name, edition, computer codes and dates) and to keep the business and tax records the law requires. We do not sell or rent it, and we do not share it with anyone except where the law requires us to.

We keep licence records for as long as the licence can be renewed or re-issued, and payment records for as long as the law requires. You may ask us at any time what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it or to delete what we no longer need.

Messages and payments pass through the services you choose to use, such as WhatsApp, your e-mail provider, JazzCash or your bank. Their own privacy policies apply to the information they handle.

3. This website and downloads

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising or tracking code. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the installer is downloaded from GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit or download, for security and to run the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive information that identifies individual visitors.

Links that open WhatsApp or your e-mail program only fill in a draft message for you; nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

4. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
