Everything a small office needs from a backup

Encrypted with AES-256

Everything is encrypted before it reaches the backup drive. If the drive is lost or stolen, nobody can see your file names or contents.

Every version kept

Each backup is a complete snapshot. Restore one file, a folder or everything, exactly as it was on any earlier date.

Fast after the first time

Only changed files are read again. Content already stored is never stored twice, even when a file is renamed or copied, and everything is compressed.

Runs by itself

Daily or weekly backups. A missed backup runs when the computer is switched on again, and a waiting one runs as soon as the USB drive is plugged in.

Checks its own backups

Integrity checks confirm your backups can really be restored, and repair them when needed. A power cut during a backup never damages earlier ones.

Completely offline

No cloud, no account, no monthly bill. Your files never leave your office, and the program never connects to the internet.

Set up once, protected every day

  1. Create a repository on your USB drive and choose a password. You also get a recovery key, in case the password is ever forgotten.
  2. Choose what to back up: Documents, Desktop, scanned papers, Outlook data and your accounting software's data folder.
  3. Turn on the schedule. Amanat Backup backs up every day and tells you if anything needs your attention.

The program's Dashboard has a getting-started checklist, and help is one key away (F1). Read the step-by-step guide.

Get any file back from any day

Pick a date, browse your folders as they were, or search for a file by name. Restore to another folder to compare first, or straight back to where it came from after a disk failure or a virus attack.

  • Restoring is always allowed, whatever your edition, even after a trial or licence ends.
  • Moving to a new computer? Install, connect the drive, open the repository and restore.
  • Every restored file is checked before it is put in place.
